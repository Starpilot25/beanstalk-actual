Privacy policy

Storybooks starring your child. A student project for MCON 368. Last updated {{ updated }}.

What we collect

  • Your account: if you sign in with Google, your name, email address and profile picture, which Google shares with us. If you sign in with the class password, the name you type.
  • Your books: the child's name, age, likes and "about me" text you enter, and the pictures the app generates (the hero picture, the story pages and the PDF).
  • Payments: your balance, deposits, book charges and refunds. Card details are entered on Stripe's own checkout page and never reach this site. Payments are Stripe test mode: no real money moves.
  • A usage log: when pictures are made, how long they took and whether they worked, so we can fix problems and keep costs under control.

What we don't keep

The photo you upload is used once to create the cast sheet and is never stored. We don't sell your data, show ads or use tracking cookies. The only cookie keeps you signed in.

Who else sees it

To make and check the pictures, your photo and the prompts are sent to OpenAI's API, which processes them under OpenAI's privacy policy. Payments go through Stripe. The app runs on Render, which hosts the server and database. Nobody else receives your data.

Your choices

  • Delete a book at any time with the "Delete this book" button. Its pictures and details are removed from our database.
  • To have your whole account removed, contact us{% if contact %} at {{ contact }}{% endif %}.

Children

This app is meant to be used by a parent or guardian making a book for their child. Please don't upload a photo of a child unless you're their parent or guardian or have their permission.
