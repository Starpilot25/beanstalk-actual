## Gaps in this data
- The form asked "Does Jack look like you?" and "Does it look professional?" but **not** "Does the story ring true?"
  or "What isn't good?", so nobody answered those two.
- Answers are short and all positive. Friends and family are kind; that is a known bias of this kind of sample.
